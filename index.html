Flagship · Enterprise production · 2023–2026

EY ConvoCoach: GenAI coaching at enterprise scale

In short: EY ConvoCoach is an AI powered verbal communication training platform built by EY Foundry for enterprise teams. It helps employees get better at hard workplace conversations, such as a difficult client call, a feedback meeting, or a sensitive team discussion, by practising out loud before the real conversation happens. I built this product as Product Manager, later Senior Product Manager, at EY Foundry.

What ConvoCoach is

ConvoCoach gives learners simulated role play practice in a judgement free environment. A learner talks with a virtual, prerecorded colleague and responds in their own words. There are no multiple choice answers to pick from. The AI coach listens to the spoken response, converts speech to text, and uses semantic similarity to compare what the learner said against an expert response. It then gives immediate, personalised feedback on the content of the answer, so the learner can try again and improve inside the same session. Practice is available on demand, at any time, including months after a training workshop, and organisations get a library of expert designed courses, plus admin controls, learner progress tracking, and analytics on pass rates and skills.

How it works

  • Practice in your own words: the learner hears a workplace scenario from a virtual colleague and replies out loud, using their own language.
  • Receive immediate feedback: speech is converted to text, each answer is graded by a combined machine learning and LLM system, and feedback comes back inside the session.
  • Practice when you need it: learners can repeat a conversation, track progress against a skills framework, and return for more practice before a real meeting.
  • For organisations: admins assign modules, manage licences, and see completion, progress, and performance trends across learners.

My role

I built ConvoCoach as its Product Manager, later Senior Product Manager, at EY Foundry. I owned the full product lifecycle: roadmap, releases, user acceptance testing, AI governance, client onboarding, and commercial operations. I also built ConvoCreator, the GenAI authoring tool inside ConvoCoach, which cut content creation time by 80% or more.

The decision that mattered

Use GenAI upstream, in content production, not only as a learner-facing chatbot, with human review kept in the loop. ConvoCreator takes curriculum requirements from an author, uses GPT-4o Mini to draft the script and the grading criteria, and the author reviews and edits before anything ships. For grading, we built a proprietary ML and LLM model that scores spoken answers using semantic similarity, rather than relying on an LLM alone, so scoring could be validated and governed.

Constraints

Regulated enterprise clients; AI governance (AI QRM / AIRA) before production; security remediation alongside feature work. ConvoCoach V5.2's Coach Response Module completed AI QRM review at a Limited Risk tier.

Outcome

Production platform through continued releases (V6.0 UAT submitted April 2026), formal evaluation citing 1,000+ active paid customers, grading performance progressing ~92% → 96% across documented generations, and ContentAssist opening additional business opportunities. ContentAssist in plain English: it is the GenAI authoring help inside ConvoCoach where an author gives context and curriculum requirements and the AI drafts the first version of coaching content for a human to review and edit.

Flagship · LLM platform · 2025–2026

10xAI / 10x.ai: Tax research with retrieval and evaluation

In short: 10xAI is an AI tax research tool for EY tax professionals. A researcher asks a tax question in plain language, and the system drafts the research and a structured tax memo, with sources cited, for the researcher to check and finish. It is not a plain chatbot: the model works through a tools API, calling search, retrieval, and document tools step by step, so every claim in the draft can be traced back to a source. I led the MVP, an LLM-based memo builder, and helped productise it into this enterprise tax research workflow.

The decision that mattered

Treat source quality and citation evaluation as product requirements, scoped before prompt work: user analytics, retrieval quality and human validation as the loop that improves the product, not an afterthought.

How it works

  • Question in: the researcher describes the tax issue, the jurisdiction, and the facts of the case.
  • Tools API: the LLM plans the research and calls tools through an API: search over trusted tax content, retrieval of full source documents, and citation checks. This is function calling. The model requests a tool, gets real results back, and works from those results instead of from memory.
  • Draft out: the system produces a structured memo draft with citations attached to the claims they support.
  • Human review: the tax professional verifies the sources, edits the draft, and approves it. Nothing goes to a client unchecked.
  • Evaluation loop: source quality, citation accuracy, and retrieval quality are measured, and those measures drive product improvements.

Outcome

MVP delivered and carried toward a broader enterprise tax research platform for real tax research work, with an evaluation and platform quality strategy that connects directly to Prior Work and Eval Creator (below), which supply the approved expert work the research output is tested against.

Technical detail

LLMs with a tools API (function calling) · retrieval agents that call search and document tools · RAG and retrieval over tax source libraries · AI orchestration across multi-step research tasks · data ingestion pipelines for source content · source and citation evaluation · AI evaluation frameworks · human validation · enterprise data architecture.

Prior Work Utilization + Eval Creator: governed knowledge reuse

In short: Valuable tax work already exists inside EY, in memos and work products that experts have already finished, but reusing it needs identification, approvals, controlled retrieval, redaction of client details, and review. Prior Work Utilization is the system for that: each piece of past work becomes a tracked case, from intake and qualification through approval, redaction, and human review, until it can be reused safely. We worked with other internal teams to build it as one unified database and service setup. The setup connects to internal workspaces, including the CIT intake route and EYI / MyDocs, so approved prior tax work can be pulled in directly, instead of someone gathering files by hand from different places. Eval Creator then takes that approved, redacted work and turns it into evaluation assets: test cases made from real expert work, including extracted claims, research questions, and expected sources, which tax specialists (SMEs) validate. Those assets are used to test and improve 10xAI tax research. I defined the Prior Work workflow end to end and co-led Eval Creator from proof of concept (Sept 2026) to a defined MVP.

The decision that mattered

Knowledge Gap Assessment first: before spending SME time creating a new evaluation asset, compare the current Researcher Agent's output against the approved gold work product. No meaningful gap, no new asset. That avoids duplicates, prioritises higher-value work, and gives an objective return for eval creation.

Delivered by October 2026

  • Unified database and service setup, built with other internal teams: we connected Prior Work to internal workspaces so it does not sit on its own. CIT ingestion completed: prior tax work can now come in through the CIT intake route. EYI / MyDocs integration in UAT: EYI and MyDocs are internal EY workspaces where work documents are stored, and this connection lets Prior Work pull an approved document directly from there. Together, these connections mean approved prior tax work can be pulled in directly, instead of staff gathering and uploading files by hand or relying on emailed copies. Multiple redaction improvements shipped and the front-end workflow is substantially complete.
  • Eval Creator POC with working claim extraction and front-end interactions; Admin and Reviewer workflows defined (memo review, claims review, source validation, question generation, trajectory review).
  • Human-in-the-loop model throughout: AI generates → SME reviews → SME approves, generating feedback and training data as a by-product.

Technical detail

LLM / AI redaction · Presidio-related anonymisation · APIs / ingestion · Figma · Azure DevOps · Eval Creator POC stack documented Oct 2026: .NET 8, Angular / Node, SQL Server Express, Copilot-assisted development, Figma MCP prototyping. Success framework: cases processed, SME effort reduction, source match, claim coverage, conclusion accuracy, recall / precision, jurisdiction coverage, edit rate, trajectory coverage.
